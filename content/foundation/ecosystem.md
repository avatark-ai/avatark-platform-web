## Begin
products: echo
Echo preserves the part of one life that can help another life begin. It is where the whole ecosystem starts — and, per the Founder Letter, the part not yet built.

## Practice
products: prometheusk
PrometheusK turns recurring observations and inherited wisdom into practices that can be attempted, reflected upon and improved.

## Play
products: gamek
GameK turns learning into experience through flow, paths, geometry, chronology and navigation.

## Gather
products: arenak
ArenaK gives private practice a communal dimension through challenges, cohorts, evidence, recognition and contribution.

## Watch
products: streamk, cinemak
StreamK allows journeys, practices and human stories to be witnessed and shared. CinemaK explores those same stories through enduring narrative and archetype.

## Create
products: studiok
Creation tools for the ecosystem's own content and practices, not yet integrated with the Platform.

## Understand
products: atlas, setpointk
Atlas connects knowledge, evidence, relationships and decisions. SetpointK, a wellness and goal-setting product, has not yet been integrated with Platform identity.
