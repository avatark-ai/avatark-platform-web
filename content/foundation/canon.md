---
title: The Canon
subtitle: Four Axes
---

The Canon is the geometry underneath AvatarK: three axes of inner development, drawn from Indian philosophical tradition, and a fourth — Prometheus — that turns what has been learned into something given to others. The Founder Letter tells the story of how these four axes came together; this is what each of them means.

## Awareness
figure: Adiyogi

Adiyogi represents the awakening and mastery of awareness.

## Wisdom
figure: Krishna

Krishna represents wisdom—the ability to navigate complexity, contradiction and uncertainty.

## Responsibility
figure: Rama

Rama represents responsibility: the willingness to act, uphold purpose and carry the consequences of one's choices.

## Creation
figure: Prometheus

In Greek mythology, Prometheus does not seek knowledge or power only for himself. He carries fire to humanity so that others may create, build and shape a different future. He represents creation, stewardship and contribution. For me, Prometheus became the fourth axis. Prometheus revealed the perpendicular journey: taking what we have learned and become, and using it to create value for others. Awareness without contribution can remain private. Wisdom without practice can remain abstract. Responsibility without creation can preserve the world but may not advance it. Prometheus completes the geometry by asking: What will you give forward?
