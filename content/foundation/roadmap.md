AvatarK is built in the open about where it actually is. No fixed dates below — the ecosystem's own internal roadmap deliberately treats maturity as something a product earns through real use, not something scheduled onto a calendar.

## Today
Two products are live and public: AvatarK itself and PrometheusK. GameK is in public beta. ArenaK and StreamK are reachable, public, and still labeled alpha while their maturity is decided by the people who run them. CinemaK, StudioK and Atlas are building, not yet public. SetpointK is a historical product not yet integrated with AvatarK identity. Echo — the part of the architecture every other product ultimately serves — has not been built yet.

## The maturity ladder
Every product in this ecosystem is expected to climb the same ladder, regardless of where it starts: a story worth telling, guided practices, structured challenges, peer competition, live events, an ongoing stream, produced narrative, and finally a creator ecosystem that scales content faster than any editorial team could alone. Nothing here is a promise of when a given product reaches a given rung — only a description of the shape climbing takes.
